Hey! I'm Bethany. You may also know me as B!
I am a New York City based designer and mixed media artist. My work explores intangible elements—light, sound, reflection, and time—through material experimentation and perceptual manipulation.

My childhood habit of re-arranging my shared bedroom, much to my sister's annoyance, sparked my pursuit of a Bachelor's Degree in Interior Design and Technical Theatre specializing in Scenic and Properties Design.
Currently, I am a Master's student at the Interactive Telecommunications Program (ITP) at NYU Tisch School of the Arts.

While still rooted in physical objects, my recent practice incorporates digital and computational elements to expand interactive possibilities. My work is often an invitation to slow down and reconnect with the value of handmade craft. Through a blend of digital innovation and hands-on techniques I aim to celebrate the care and skill that often gets overlooked in our fast-paced world.

2025
Chronoscope
Product
Designer & Developer
.
"Chronoscope" is a handheld object that draws from 1990s childhood nostalgia, carrying the visual language of Fisher Price and the playfulness of the View-Master. This device is not about time keeping or taking photos, but instead explores a method of linear storytelling through tactility and imperfect narratives.

This 3D printed object features a custom designed dual film cartridge that is loaded with two rolls of film printed on acetate, which can be personalized. The two rolls of film are not indexed, meaning they may advance at slightly different rates depending on the tension of the winding action, creating a parallax effect.

"How To Use" instruction video edited by Nasif Rincon Romaite.
"Assmebly" instruction video edited by Bethany "B" Wu.

This piece was exhibited in March 2026 at Clive Davis Gallery in Brooklyn, NY as part of a group exhibition Perspective, produced by Bethany "B" Wu, curated by Audrey DOh, hebin rachel shin, Jiyou Park, Nasif Rincon Romaite, Nikolai Kozak.

Cordless Tether
Interactive Art
Artist & Exhibit Producer
.
"Cordless Tether" is a constructed archive of memories surrounding the summer of 2003, housed in a Panasonic cordless phone. This work is an audio collage of phone call interviews with 2 sets of sisters: my mom, my aunt Ariel “ayi”; my sister Brianna, and me, as we recount our own memories of a migratory transition. The conversations contextualize our feelings of loss and disconnection through a device that kept us tethered to each other.

This work was exhibited in November 2025 at All Street Gallery in New York, NY as part of a group exhibition Perimeter, produced by Bethany "B" Wu, curated by hebin rachel shin, Jiyou Park, Nasif Rincon Romaite, Nikolai Kozak.

2025
Flocking | Dumbo Projection Project, Vol. Six
Projection Art
Co-Creator in collaboration with Audrey Oh and Sai Ram Ved Vijapurapu
.
"Flocking" is a site-specific projection artwork created for the DUMBO Projection Project Volume 6: Unexpected Delight. This piece explores how industrial infrastructure intersects with the natural ecosystem in the area. Taking inspiration from DUMBO's distinctive soundscape, animated birds respond dynamically to the rumble of passing trains. As trains pass, the birds scatter in flight, and the wires they once rested upon transform into rhythmic waves synchronized with the train's noise. The birds return only when the soundscape settles, creating a rhythm of disruption and restoration.

Conductivity Testing Gadgets
Product
Designer & Creator
.
These wearable conductivity testers are created for discrete testing when sourcing project materials. Each tester explores an aspect of gadgetry that I find fascinating and hope to explore further in the future.

The cuff tester features probe interchangeability through conductive snap button attachment. The necklace tester explores alternative probe options and almost hands-free carrying experience. The glove tester provides non-visual feedback - when the circuit is closed, a vibe motor is triggered to provide haptic feedback to the user.

This collection was showcased at the 2025 NYC Resistor Wearables Open Mic.

Reveal, Generate, Blend | NYU ITP Spring Show 2025
Exhibition Design
Co-Creator in collaboration with Sky Seo
.
“Reveal, Generate, Blend” is a playful and educational activity based exhibit about additive color - also known as color mixing with light. Through playful interactions, visitors will learn about additive color, make creative decisions, and generate their own unique light painting. This exhibit encourages exploration to deepen existing understanding of color and expand knowledge of light.

2024
Where You Linger | NYU ITP Winter Show 2024
Interactive Art
Designer & Creator
.
"Where You Linger" is an interactive diorama that explores our personal relationships with time when we are engaged with our work. Inside the diorama, which the user sees through a peephole, is a Pepper's Ghost projection of a person engaging in different activities at a desk. The user is able to alter the passage of time within the diorama, which reflects how fleeting time can be when we are focused on a task at hand. This creates a different experience for each user depending on where they choose to linger in the model.

2024
Untitled Light & Color Exploration
Interactive Art
Co-Creator in collaboration with Julia Xu
.
This project explores the manipulation of light transmission as a controller for soundwaves. As ink is added to a body of water, the light that is read from a sensor below the vessel alters and in turn changes the amplitude of the soundwaves. The more obscured the water, the sounds become quieter.

2023
YosekaLAB: two-month weekly planner | Yoseka Stationery
Product
Co-creator in collaboration with Amy Hong
.
The "YOSEKALAB Two-Month Weekly Planner" is a 32-page, staple bound, B6 cahier with a tan Kraft cover and Tomoe River Paper interior pages. The planner features 4 memo pages, 3 monthly spreads, and 10 weekly spreads (6 designs); with an insert that includes a user guide and planner matrix for ease of reference to related products. While the product serves as a tester for other planners, this is also a self contained product that functions as a short term planner that can be used to document travels or projects over the course of 70+ days.

2022
Yoseka Ceramics Watercolors | Yoseka Stationery
Product
Designer and creator of custom watercolor paints for Yoseka Stationery
.
The "Yoseka Ceramic Watercolors" are custom blended watercolor paints that color match the "Yoseka Ceramic Fountain pen inks". Each color is a custom blend of multiple pigments, blended with watercolor medium, then hand mulled and mixed for a near perfect match to the fountain pen ink and highlight their individual unique qualities. This was a limited release for a special event - Travel and Sketch - in collaboration with Traveler's Notebook.
