Physical Computing - Fall 2024

Course Description

This course explores the intersection of physical and digital realms through interactive projects that respond to human touch, movement, and environmental conditions.

Learning Objectives

  • Understanding sensors and actuators
  • Microcontroller programming with Arduino
  • Circuit design and prototyping
  • Interactive system design
